# Freight rate report: evidence and content contract

The final report is generated by `python -m src.build_report` from
`artifacts/metrics.json` and `reports/data_audit.json`. The builder normalizes
those records into reader-facing `artifacts/report_data.json`. Every number
comes from the recorded audit, training metrics or scorer output. No unseen
validation label is available; the official scorer verifies output structure
and produces the chart.

## Intended six-page report

1. **Approach and outcome:** target, selected model, exact temporal holdout
   metrics, key findings, scope of evidence.
2. **Data and preparation:** development/validation/December rows and dates;
   missing/invalid weights, missing market signal, duplicates/identifiers,
   novel locations/lanes and treatments.
3. **Features and model:** model-independent transformations; numerical,
   categorical, geography and calendar features; target-sensitive quote/market
   assumptions; fit-only-on-training rule; model rationale.
4. **Chronological evaluation:** precise split dates and row counts, meaningful
   baseline, candidate scores, choice criteria and held-out subgroup diagnostics.
5. **December chart:** the complete official `candidate_december.png`, unchanged;
   reduced feature contract; how missing market/quote/geography are handled;
   absence of observed December labels.
6. **Reproducibility and limits:** runnable commands, deliverable paths, scorer
   checks, reproducibility identifiers; GitHub/Loom remain pending user actions.

## `artifacts/report_data.json` contract

The generated intermediate contains these objects, with strings formatted for
readers. Its purpose is report provenance and layout separation, rather than
an independently maintained set of performance numbers:

```text
summary:
  subtitle: str
  cards: [{value: str, label: str}, ...]
  decision: [str, ...]
  findings: [str, ...]
  caveat: str
data:
  introduction: str
  scope_headers: [str x4]
  scope_rows: [[str x4], ...]
  quality_headers: [str x3]
  quality_rows: [[str x3], ...]
  notes: [str, ...]
features:
  introduction: str
  headers: [str x3]
  rows: [[str x3], ...]
  rationale: [str, ...]
  availability: [str, ...]
  caveat: str
evaluation:
  introduction: str
  split_headers: [str x3]
  split_rows: [[str x3], ...]
  result_headers: [str, ...]
  result_rows: [[str, ...], ...]
  result_widths: [float, ...] # optional fractions summing to 1
  notes: [str, ...]
  diagnostic_headers: [str, ...] # optional
  diagnostic_rows: [[str, ...], ...] # optional
  interpretation: [str, ...] # optional
december:
  introduction: str
  chart_path: "reports/scorer_results/candidate_december.png"
  caption: str
  method: [str, ...]
  caveat: str
reproducibility:
  introduction: str
  artifact_headers: [str x2]
  artifact_rows: [[str x2], ...]
  commands: [str, ...]
  verification: [str, ...]
  limitations: [str, ...]
  provenance: str
```

The report builder escapes JSON content, uses selectable text and standard
fonts, wraps table cells, numbers pages, and preserves the chart aspect ratio.
`--render-dir reports/pdf_preview` renders headless QA previews with PyMuPDF.
Any overflow beyond six pages must be reviewed before delivery.
